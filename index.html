About Their Work

Every quarter, one part of the business quietly hits a wall: a process that takes too long, a handoff that loses information, a report that ten people rebuild from scratch every week. For one of our delivery teams, that wall was hiding inside a workflow everyone had learned to live with — until Jane decided that "this is just how it's done" wasn't a good enough answer.

Rather than patching the symptom, Jane spent time actually watching how the work happened — where it stalled, who waited on whom, and which steps existed only because "that's how it's always been." From there, she designed and built a lightweight automation layer that removed the manual busywork without removing the judgment calls that still needed a human. It shipped in weeks, not quarters, and it was built to be handed off, not babysat.

The impact showed up fast: hours returned to the team every week, fewer errors slipping through, and a template other groups have since asked to borrow. But what earned Jane this feature isn't just the tool — it's the habit behind it. She treats "we've always done it this way" as a question, not an answer, and that mindset is exactly what BT Signal wants more people across the community to see.
